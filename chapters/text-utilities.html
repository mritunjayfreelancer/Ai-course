Text utilities ka practical use

Chhote text tasks ko manually karne ki zarurat kam karo.

01

Analyze before editing

Pehle words, characters, lines aur reading time dekho. Isse content ki basic size aur structure samajh aata hai.

02

Clean before using

Extra spaces aur unnecessary blank lines remove karne se copied ya generated text ko organize karna easy hota hai.

03

Format consistently

Case conversion aur line formatting repetitive editing ko fast aur consistent bana sakti hai.

04

Remove duplicates

Lists, copied data aur repeated entries me duplicate lines ko quickly identify aur remove karo.

05

Find exactly what matters

Find and replace ka use karke repeated words, names ya formatting patterns ko quickly change karo.

06

Keep your workflow local

Normal text operations ke liye external service ki zarurat nahi. Processing browser me hi ho sakti hai.
